## 🚀 Overview

**RegionLearn-Bridge** is a mobile-first vernacular AI tutoring platform engineered specifically for Grade 9 & 10 secondary students in rural India preparing for state board examinations.

In rural classrooms plagued by severe teacher deficits (**PTR > 1:45**) and an intimidating English language barrier (**85% of STEM content in English**), over **55% of students fail** not from a lack of intelligence, but from undetected prerequisite gaps in Grades 6–8. 

RegionLearn-Bridge delivers a **"Duolingo + Khan Academy powered by an AI tutor in your mother tongue"** — a 24/7 personal village schoolmaster inside a family phone for less than **₹99/month**.
